## 4. Verified observations vs hypotheses

Verified facts:

1. The 90.1 s first-decision deadline fired while the orchestrator held
   decision-1; this is a Pipeline-internal (Studio) deadline baked into the
   frozen Baseline v2 binary — the same binary and budget that P4-C used for
   six slots without ever triggering it.
2. The orchestrator delivered exactly one non-ACTION response at ~55 s.
3. That reply was accompanied by an anomalous turn shape: ToolUse blocks with
   `stop_reason=EndTurn` (studio.log WARN).

Recorded hypotheses (causality NOT established — do not assume random
provider latency either):

- The reply's wording ("I'll review the workspace first, then choose the
  next action") is consistent with the orchestrator planning a multi-turn
  inspection before emitting an ACTION, while a decision task expects a
  single-turn ACTION.
- The EndTurn/ToolUse anomaly may have truncated the turn before an ACTION
  could form. The warning proves the turn shape was irregular; it does not
  prove it caused the missing ACTION. The invalid ACTION, the warning, and
  the timeout may share an upstream cause (e.g. provider-side behavior) or
  be independent; this is unresolved.
- The 90 s decision budget is the value in the frozen binary P4-D is pinned
  to; the live working tree's `ORCHESTRATOR_DECISION_TIMEOUT = 180 s`
  (`pipeline.rs:51`, uncommitted, modified after the freeze) never executed.
